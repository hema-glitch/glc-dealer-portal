How Schemes Work
💸 Cash Discount — 3%
Dealers earn 3% discount when they pay the full invoice amount within 15 days of invoice date.

Standard: 15 days window | 3%
Premium: 20 days window | 3%

How credit works: When a dealer qualifies, your team creates a Credit Note in Zoho Books for 3% of the invoice amount. This credit is applied against their next invoice automatically.
🏆 Quarterly Rebate — 3% / 4%
Calculated on total purchases in a 90-day cycle (quarterly).

Standard: 3% rebate | AED 10,000 cap
Premium: 4% rebate | AED 15,000 cap

How credit works: At cycle end, review the Rebate Tracker below → create a Credit Note in Zoho Books for the earned amount → apply to next invoice or pay directly.
🎁 FOC (Free of Cost)
Buy X get Y free — configured per product in schemeEngine.js.
Example: Buy 10 units → get 2 free.

How it works: When a dealer places an order with eligible products, FOC units are added as zero-rate line items in the Zoho Books Sales Order automatically.
